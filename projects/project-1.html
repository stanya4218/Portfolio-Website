Worldmark 2.0 is a $2B, 3.5M sq ft LEED-certified mixed-use commercial development in Aerocity, New Delhi — designed as a 24/7 global business district. The development comprises 3 basements, ground, and 8 upper floors, housing office, retail, and hospitality uses within a single integrated masterplan.

I was involved across the full project lifecycle — from concept development through jurisdiction approvals, construction documents, and site execution, through to handover. This is one of the few projects where I have seen every phase of a large-scale commercial development from the inside.

A key contribution during design development was driving a floor plate efficiency increase that directly improved the project's overall rentable area — a design change coordinated and resolved during an active development stage, across multiple stakeholder teams.
